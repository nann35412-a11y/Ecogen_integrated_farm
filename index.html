Our Charter

"As the world becomes increasingly interdependent and fragile, the future at once holds great peril and great promise. We are one human family and one Earth community with a common destiny." — Preamble, UN's Earth Charter Initiative, 2000

Vision

An Earth in its fullness is fullness of life for all — a nurtured environment, a healthy existence for all.

Mission

Care of the Earth and all that live in it — to cultivate a mutuality between the environment and the human community.

Who We Are

Empowering people through education, training, and action

Ecogenesis is an organization that aims to empower individuals and groups through education, training and action. Our mission focuses on the environment, local communities, farmers, indigenous people, youth, women & children, and churches & schools.

We are committed to the empowerment of individuals and local communities who face environmental issues, and to preserving cultural, religious, and indigenous earth wisdom.

Ecogenesis Farm & Training Center

A permaculture-designed farm, from the ground up

Located in Purok 1, Brgy. Balete, Diadi, Nueva Vizcaya, our farm follows the principles of Permaculture Design — a dynamic, mutual relationship between the land, the farmer, and the community. Explore our sweet potato project, eco-village, off-grid solar system, and training center.
