Why Support Ecogenesis

Every gift trains a steward of the land

Ecogenesis awards scholarships to those interested in education, training and practice of permaculture farming, wellness, community organizing, leadership, stewardship, and eco-conscious economy. It funds small-scale projects for women and youth, indigenous people, churches & schools, and farmers.

Your support helps maintain our model permaculture-designed garden, conduct site visits and consultations, and run our training center for wellness, alternative medicine, and healthy living.
